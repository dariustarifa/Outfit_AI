For Outfit's dark appearance, keep `#B2B2B2` as the background and `#555779` for outlines and primary controls. Use dark ink on the gray background and reserve `#F4F4F4` for text on darker surfaces.

**Why:** The user chose to preserve the exact gray background after learning that `#F4F4F4` over `#B2B2B2` has only 1.93:1 contrast.

**How to apply:** Keep this palette priority in the design-system tokens and preserve accessible contrast when changing dark-theme roles.
